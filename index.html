Get started

Up and running in two steps.

Pair your buddy with the Pay Buddy Pro app, then make it yours.

1Turn on & pair your device

  1. Press the button once while the device is off.The LED blinks red and the buzzer sounds.
  2. Press the button once more.The LED blinks blue and green — a 30-second pairing window is open.
  3. In the app, go to Home → Add Device.The app scans for nearby Pay Buddy Pro devices over Bluetooth.
  4. Select your device, enter a nickname (up to 50 characters), optionally assign a profile, then tap Pair Device.

2Personalise & sync an image

  1. Open the Customize tab and choose an image source.Preset for built-in images, Import for your own photos, or Saved for designs you've made before.
  2. Crop and position your image.
  3. Add text and set its colour, position and font style, and choose an image tone.
  4. Tap Sync To Device and pick a paired device.
  5. Choose one of the 6 numbered slots, then tap Sync To A Slot.
After the success message, allow 20–25 seconds for the e-ink screen to refresh.
Smart Buddy

Insert your payment chip.

If you've applied for a Smart Buddy payment chip from POSB, here's how to fit it.

1Open the chip tray

  • Find the small groove on the side of the device — it's a groove, not a pinhole.
  • Insert a pin or similar thin tool into the groove, hook it against the tray and pull.
  • If your tool won't fit, try a thinner, more rigid one such as a straightened paperclip or a SIM tool.
Use only the groove. Don't force or pry at other edges of the tray.

2Insert the chip

  • Place the POSB payment chip in the tray so it sits flat.
  • Push the tray in fully until it's flush with the device body and the red seal is no longer visible.

Tap to pay

Hold your Pay Buddy Pro against any contactless payment terminal. Payment is handled entirely by the built-in chip and card slot — the Pay Buddy Pro app never collects, stores or processes your payment credentials. To view payment features, use the Smart Buddy app.

Find My & Find Hub

Never lose your buddy.

Pay Buddy Pro works with Apple Find My on iPhone and Google Find Hub on Android. Set it up from your phone's system app — not from inside Pay Buddy Pro.

Pair with Find My or Find Hub

  1. Power on the device.An unpaired device advertises for pairing for 5 minutes, then stops.
  2. In Apple Find My or Google Find Hub, add a new item and select your device.
  3. Give it a name, choose an icon and confirm.The device beeps once and flashes blue to confirm. Finding stays available as long as the battery has charge.
Apple Find My requires iOS 26.0 or later (iPadOS 26.0 or later on iPad) for firmware V5.0.0-R20 and later. Earlier versions may not pair or work reliably. Only one network — Apple or Google — can be active on a device at a time.

Play a sound

Within Bluetooth range of your phone, tap Play Sound in Find My or Find Hub. Your buddy beeps and flashes blue (3 s on, 1 s off) for 60 seconds, or until you stop it from your phone.

Locate from the app

Open Locate Device in Pay Buddy Pro for a live "nearby" status, with a shortcut to Apple Find My (iOS) or Google Find Hub (Android).

Remove the device

  • In Find My or Find Hub, select the device and choose Remove Item.
  • Keep the device next to your phone — it beeps once when removed.
  • If it wasn't nearby, you'll need to factory-reset it before it can join another Apple or Google account.

Turn off finding

Power the device off — press the button four times quickly. It beeps three times and flashes red, and Find My / Find Hub is disabled until you power it on again.

The app

Organise, customise, control.

Everything works offline, with no account required.

Profiles

  • Group devices by person or purpose in the Users tab. Each profile gets a name and one of 20 emoji icons.
  • Create up to 50 profiles, each with one or more devices.
  • Rename a profile and its devices follow automatically.
  • Deleting a profile keeps its devices — they move to "No Profile".

Devices

  • Pair up to 100 devices.
  • Tap a device in Users to Customise, Locate Device, Assign To Profile, open the Device Guide, or Unpair Device.
  • Unpairing removes a device from the app's local data and can't be undone.

Settings

  • Language — English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu or Japanese. Applies instantly.
  • Appearance — Light, Dark or System Default.
  • Permissions — see Bluetooth, Camera and Photos status, and jump to system Settings.

Wipe all local data

Permanently deletes all profiles, saved images and sync history, returning the app to its first-launch state. You'll be asked to confirm twice. Your OS permissions and language stay as they are.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an internet connection or account?

No. Every core feature — onboarding, scanning, editing and syncing — works entirely offline, with no login.

Why isn't my device appearing in the scan list?

Make sure it's powered on and in pairing mode, Bluetooth is on, and you're within about 5 m. Only supported Pay Buddy Pro hardware appears in results.

What is a slot?

One of 6 numbered image memory locations on the device. Hold the button for 2 seconds to cycle through them.

Why does my image look different on the device?

The e-ink screen has a fixed colour palette. Your saved image stays full-colour in the app — a converted copy is generated automatically when you sync.

Does the app handle payments?

No. Smart Buddy NFC payment is handled entirely by the device's own hardware.

Are my saved images backed up?

No — they're stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or wiping its data deletes them permanently.

Can I use the app with Bluetooth off?

Yes — you can browse and edit images. You just can't scan or sync until Bluetooth is back on.

How is my device located if it's lost?

When the device is separated from you, the Find My or Find Hub network uses nearby compatible devices to help locate it.

How does it discourage unwanted tracking?

If a device that isn't with its owner is seen moving with you over time, you'll be notified. On iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 14.5 or later you get a system notification; without a compatible phone, the device itself plays a sound when moved.

On Android, Find Hub alerts you to an unfamiliar device moving with you, and lets you see it on a map, play a sound to find it, or get help.

How is my location privacy protected?

Your device's location data is stored securely so that only you can see it. Neither Apple, Google nor any other person or company can view or access it.

What is the Find My / Find Hub network?

A simple, secure way to see a paired device on a map in Find My (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Find Hub (Android), alongside your other devices. If it's lost, turn on Lost Mode to show a message and contact details to whoever finds it. The network is encrypted and anonymous.

How do I disable Find My / Find Hub?

Power the device off by pressing the button four times quickly while it's on. The red LED flashes once and it beeps three times, confirming shutdown — Find My / Find Hub is disabled at the same time.

How do I factory reset my device?

Double-click the button, then press and hold until you hear a chord tone and the LED flashes about once per second. Release the button — the reset is complete. This is required before joining a different Apple or Google account if the device wasn't removed while next to the original phone.

Safety & regulatory

Good to know.

Battery & charging safety

  • Charge only within the rated input (5 V ⎓ 0.15 A max) and within the specified temperature range.
  • Align the magnetic charging contacts correctly before charging.
  • Keep charging contacts away from keys, coins and other metal objects to avoid short circuits.
  • The lithium battery is built in — don't disassemble, puncture, crush, bend, replace or modify it or the device.
  • Keep away from fire, heat sources and extreme temperatures. Never dispose of it in a fire.
  • Stop using and charging immediately if it overheats, swells, deforms, smells unusual or smokes.
  • Unauthorised modification of circuitry, battery, charging structure or safety protections is prohibited.
  • At end of life, recycle the device and battery according to local regulations.
